💌 Dear Lady

Write down your worries, feelings, or story. Your letter gets sent privately to my inbox and I will write you back.

💭 4 Deep Questions for Today

Take 3 quiet minutes to ask yourself these four gentle questions today:

1. What made you feel heavy today?

Acknowledge it without judgment, then take a deep breath out.

2. What is one tiny thing you did well?

Even if it was just waking up, resting, or being gentle with yourself.

3. What are you holding onto that isn't yours to carry?

Someone else's mood, expectations, or opinion?

4. What does your heart need right now?

A glass of water, a quiet moment, or encouraging words?
